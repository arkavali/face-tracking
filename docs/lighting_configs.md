## Pixel-metric sweeps (layouts lit together, no trained net)

- `tools/ledsim.py` (round 1): 137 spots, x 0/15/30/45, y 70-150, z 270-330 mm absolute -> `led_report.txt`, `led_layouts.png`; blinking check `tools/ledsim2ch.py` -> `led_report_2ch.txt`.
- `tools/ledsweep2.py` (round 2): 769 spots, x 0/10/20/30/45, y slit-40..+60, z 265-340, wall bounce -> `led2_report.txt`, `led2_layouts.png`, `led2_map.png`; wider pairs `--xs 45,55,65,75 --hpairs-only --out led2_wide` -> `led2_wide_report.txt`.
- These cannot judge blinking: use `synth.py` with a trained net for that.

## Adding an idea

1. Add LED spots + variants as a new entry of `SETS` in `tools/synth.py` (or a variant to an existing set: its frames are already rendered, only training is needed).
2. Keep LEDs in free space and outside the camera view (this catalogue flags `in camera view`).
3. Render (~10 min for 6000 frames x 10 LEDs), train (~16 s per run), copy the report to docs/ and add it to `REPORTS` here, run this script.
4. Keep a reference variant (e.g. `ref blink U1/D1`, `ref both U1+D1`) in every new set.
